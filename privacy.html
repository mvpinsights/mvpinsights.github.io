Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Summary

MVPInsights runs locally on your PC. We do not operate servers, do not require an account, and do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Game data

  • During a League of Legends match the app reads the official live game data provided through Overwolf: champions, items, levels and scores of all players (the information visible on the in-game scoreboard) and the damage you take.
  • This data is analyzed on your device to show item options. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Stored on your device

  • Settings (language, overlay size and mode).
  • Match history for the post-game review: your champion, match duration, the enemy damage split over time and the items you bought. Up to 20 matches; other players' names are not stored. You can delete it at any time in the app (“Clear history”).

Network requests

  • Public game data (items, champions, icons) from Riot Games' Data Dragon (ddragon.leagueoflegends.com).
  • Augment icons from CommunityDragon (raw.communitydragon.org).
  • These requests contain no personal data beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address), which these services process under their own policies.

Advertising

The free version shows ads in the desktop window only (never in-game), delivered by Overwolf. Overwolf and its advertising partners may process data according to the Overwolf Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics

The app can create a diagnostics report to help fix problems. It stays on your PC and is only shared if you copy and send it yourself. Player names are removed from the report before it is created.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/mvpinsights/mvpinsights.github.io/issues.
