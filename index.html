MVPInsights

Adaptive, explainable item options for League of Legends – based on the enemy team you are actually playing against.

Overwolf app · in development

How it works

1 · Read the scoreboard

During a match, MVPInsights reads the same information you see on the in-game scoreboard: champions, items, levels and scores – plus the damage you take.

2 · Understand the threat

It builds a profile of the enemy team: physical / magic / true damage split, healing, crowd control, crit, shields, armor and magic resist.

3 · Choose your item

You get several suitable options – defensive, offensive and counter – each with a short reason. You always make the decision.

Fair play

  • Only official data sources (Live Client Data, Data Dragon)
  • Only information visible in the game client
  • Free for all players
  • No ads in-game or in the Riot Client
  • No hidden information (cooldowns, timers, fog of war)
  • No automation – it never buys items for you
  • No memory reading or client modification
  • No MMR / ELO calculation

Privacy

MVPInsights runs locally on your PC. Match data is analyzed on your device and is not uploaded or stored on our servers. The app only downloads public game data (items, champions, icons) from Riot's Data Dragon.
